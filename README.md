PROBLEM STATEMENT: 

Many young people in Afghanistan struggle to find opportunities such as jobs, internships, scholarships and online courses. Information is scattered across social media and different platforms making it hard and time consuming to find relevant opportunities. This app solves this problem by bringing all opportunities into one clean, searchable and easy to use platform built specifically for Afghan youth.

TARGET USERS:

This app is built for:

- 🎓 **Students** — looking for internships and courses to build skills
- 👨‍🎓 **Fresh Graduates** — searching for their first job or opportunity
- 💼 **Job Seekers** — finding full-time or part-time employment
- 👩‍💻 **Women** — looking for remote and online opportunities from home
- 📚 **Scholarship Seekers** — finding funded education opportunities
- 🌐 **Remote Workers** — finding online work from anywhere in Afghanistan
- 🏢 **Organizations** — who want to share opportunities with Afghan youth
